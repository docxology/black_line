## Contract decisions

- Preserve `refresh_horizon` as observation expiry, including non-applicable
  and shadowed occurrences, because existing valid-input tests establish that
  broader behavior. Add `effective_refresh_queue` for applicable, unshadowed
  expiry predictions with structured exclusions and exact surface transitions.
  A surface change need not change the overall status.
- Preserve strict evaluator staleness (`age > window`). In this minor release,
  `days_until_stale` counts through the first stale day. `last_fresh_on`,
  `stale_on`, and `days_until_last_fresh_day` expose the boundary and retain
  the old arithmetic under its accurate name.
