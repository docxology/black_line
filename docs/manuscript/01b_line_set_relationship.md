# Relationship to the line set

Black Line is the positive-method work in the historical four-line core.
The current family includes eight instruments, their reader and witness register;
this section orients the historical core without importing sibling semantics.
Each of the four is
its own repository:
[red_line](https://github.com/docxology/red_line) is the personal security
boundary and explicit No document;
[golden_line](https://github.com/docxology/golden_line) is the aspirational
thread; [white_line](https://github.com/docxology/white_line) is the absence
ledger: what is unknown, what is withheld under restraint, and what is left open
rather than closed with a claim. Black Line
does not copy their registries, evaluators, or manuscript claims, and its
practice findings never grant permission to cross Red Line.

Black Line records declared coverage of established work practices. Green Line's
capacity-under-development scope is distinct: Black's families are neither
capacity stages nor a progression ladder, and their statuses cannot be combined.

One word is deliberately shared with Red Line, and a reader holding only this
paper should know the other sense exists. Both papers publish a status spelled
`OUTSIDE_SCOPE`. Here it marks an attempt outside this discipline's evaluation
scope — the registry did not look, because the attempt is not the kind of thing
its practices assess. In Red Line it marks a complete, evidenced intake that
implicates no red line — that instrument did look and found no prohibition. The
spelling is shared by declaration; the two senses are not interchangeable.

The companion [line_set](https://github.com/docxology/line_set) reader checks
shared declarations while keeping native instrument semantics separate. It was
the fifth work alongside the original four; that ordinal is historical, not the
size of the current family. Black Line does not import, depend on, or defer to it.

## Note on the name

The original four colors are not decorative. Black, White, Gold, and Red openly
echo the stages of the alchemical *magnum opus*, and this paper is the black one:
*nigredo*, the blackening — the classical opening stage of dissolution and of
confronting what is base or unrefined. The register is strictly symbolic and
psychological, in the sense of Carl Jung's reading of alchemy as a map of
individuation: the blackening is the honest breakdown that must precede any
clarification, the discipline of looking squarely at raw material before
dressing it up. Nothing in the name is an empirical, mystical, or causal claim;
the alchemy is a metaphor for a posture of work, not a theory of matter or mind.
The historical core's working order — refuse (Red), then method (Black), then
aspire (Gold), then absence (White) — is a functional orientation, not a required
workflow or a progression through the current family. It deliberately does not
reenact the opus's sequence (nigredo → albedo → citrinitas → rubedo). Readers who want the full
framing, and the single Jung citation that grounds it, should consult the
companion `line_set` work at
[github.com/docxology/line_set](https://github.com/docxology/line_set), where the
alchemical layer is documented once for the whole set.

The same deterministic builder that draws the paper's figures also draws the
title-page cover, so the visual argument is versioned with the method rather
than commissioned around it. It is reproduced here because a cover printed
without its caption states nothing a reader can check:

that the work is true, safe, or authorized.
